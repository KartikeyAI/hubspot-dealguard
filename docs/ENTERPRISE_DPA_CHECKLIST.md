# Enterprise DPA and subprocessor checklist

Before public release:

- Counsel-reviewed data-processing agreement
- Current privacy policy
- Subprocessor list covering Cloudflare, HubSpot, Dodo Payments, Slack, Microsoft, Resend and any object-storage provider
- Processing purpose and data categories
- Retention and deletion commitments
- Security measures
- Incident notification terms
- International transfer terms where applicable
- Customer request process
- Version and effective date
