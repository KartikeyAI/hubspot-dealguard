# Enterprise incident response

1. Detect and classify the incident.
2. Assign incident commander and technical owner.
3. Contain affected integrations or scheduled processors.
4. Preserve logs and audit evidence.
5. Communicate through the status channel and support contacts.
6. Recover service using tested rollback or restore procedures.
7. Reconcile missed or duplicate external events.
8. Complete root-cause analysis and corrective actions.
9. Publish customer communication where required.
