# Enterprise policy management

## Lifecycle

- Template or published-policy clone
- Draft
- Simulation
- Submission
- Approval or rejection
- Publication
- Supersession
- Rollback

## Segmentation

Policies can target pipeline, stage, team, owner, region, deal type and amount bands. The deterministic evaluator resolves the most specific applicable published policy and records the policy identity on the assessment.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require an issue, reason, requester, decision, optional evidence and expiry. Approved exceptions can be revoked and automatically expire.

## Change control

Published policy changes require configured approvals and cannot be applied through general settings. Policy history, checksums and before/after diffs remain auditable.
