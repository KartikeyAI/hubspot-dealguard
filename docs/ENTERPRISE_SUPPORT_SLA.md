# Enterprise support and service objectives

## Support targets

- Security incident or widespread outage: initial response within four business hours
- Blocking production defect: one business day
- Non-blocking defect: two business days
- General configuration request: two business days

Contracted Enterprise support may define stricter targets.

## Product service objectives

Targets must be measured for:

- API availability
- Webhook acceptance
- Scheduled scan completion
- Workflow-action execution
- Outbound delivery
- Billing event processing

The production status page and incident history must state actual measured performance rather than presenting these targets as guarantees before monitoring is operational.
